A Centuries-Old Heritage: Buldan

Buldan fabric is not just a textile; it is a deep rooted cultural heritage passed down through generations since ancient times, blending hand craftsmanship and naturalness. With its labor intensive nature and centuries old history, this craft has been produced uninterrupted since the 14th century and holds a very special place today under UNESCO's intangible cultural heritage criteria. Renowned for its unique texture and quality, this fabric was used in sultan robes and palace garments during the Ottoman Empire, preserving its naturalness and prestige for centuries.

The biggest secret behind this unique weave is that no synthetic materials are used in its production, and its fibers are made entirely of pure cotton. Every thread woven on traditional handlooms gives the fabric its breathable, sweat free, and non restrictive structure. Thanks to the perfect balance of warp and weft, the natural pores in the fiber structure allow your skin to breathe. Unlike industrial fabrics, it does not stiffen when washed; instead, it softens over time, maintaining its skin friendly, flowing form for years.

As dal pure, we reinterpret this centuries old heritage with today’s simple, comfortable, and intimate style. From shirts, pants, and kimonos designed for men and women, to bags and peshtemals that complement beach culture, this selection is crafted to bring that unique freshness to your skin whenever the sun shines. More than just clothing, we want to bring the honest and pure story of centuries into your daily life in its simplest form.
